TerraWars Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE] · Applies to the TerraWars app on Android and iOS

The short version. TerraWars uses your precise location only while a walk is recording — including when your screen is off during that walk — to draw your route and work out the territory you claim. Other players never see your route or your live position; they see only the finished territory and your public username and stats. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps. Raw location points are deleted 30 days after each walk, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

3. What other players can see

Other signed-in players can see: your username, your territory colour, the territory (map areas) you hold and when it was claimed, and your game statistics (total area, number of parcels and walks, area taken and lost, rank). They cannot see your routes, your walk history, where a walk started, or your live position.

A tip about home. Territory is public. If you start and finish a walk at your front door, your home becomes a corner of a public area on the map. Start and end your walks a street away if you'd rather not reveal where you live.

4. How long we keep it

  • Raw location points are deleted automatically 30 days after the walk they belong to.
  • A simplified version of each walk's route (visible only to you) and your walk records are kept while your account exists.
  • Territory, scores and account details are kept while your account exists.
  • A walk in progress, and a finished walk waiting for a connection, are stored on your phone until they are sent — at most 24 hours for a waiting walk.
  • When you delete your account, see section 7.

5. Who we share it with

6. How we protect it

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to each player's walks and location points is restricted by database security rules so that only that player's account can read them. On your phone, the app's data is excluded from device backups and transfers.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. You can also request deletion without the app at terrawars.app/delete-account [confirm URL].

  • Immediately: all raw location points from your walks and your walk routes are erased, and your optional profile details are cleared.
  • Within 7 days: your account, email address, username, walk records, claims, statistics and raid history are permanently deleted.
  • Territory you held stays on the map so other players' maps don't develop holes, but it is reassigned to an anonymous “deleted player” and is no longer connected to you in any way.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Location: you can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your phone's settings. You won't be able to record walks without it, but the rest of the app still works.
  • Access, correction, deletion and a copy of your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • Legal bases (where GDPR applies): performance of our contract with you (running the game you signed up for), and our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and fair (anti-cheat and logs).

9. Children

TerraWars is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old (or older where local law requires) to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
