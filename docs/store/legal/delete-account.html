support@terrawars.app [confirm address]

What is deleted

  • Immediately: all raw GPS location points from your walks, your walk routes, and optional profile details.
  • Within 7 days: your account and email address, username, walk records, claims, statistics, rankings and raid history.

What is kept

  • Map territory you held stays on the shared map so other players' maps stay intact, but it is reassigned to an anonymous “deleted player” and is no longer linked to you.
  • Server security logs (such as IP addresses) are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period, then deleted automatically.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
